### 2. Short version

BRASSLIGHT is an offline game. **We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.** The game has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. We do not operate game servers.

### 3. What stays on your device

The game stores your progress, settings (for example language, volume, controls, graphics quality) and unlocks in a local save file on your device. This data is not sent to us. We cannot access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or delete the save file. Whether the operating system includes this file in device backups is controlled by your device settings.

### 4. Platform services (optional, provided by the platforms)

The game can use features of the store or platform you bought it on. These are operated by the platform provider, **not by us**. We receive no personal data from them. Their own privacy policies apply:

| Platform | Feature in the game | Provider and policy |
|----------|--------------------|---------------------|
| Steam (Windows, macOS, Linux, Steam Deck) | Steam Cloud save sync, achievements | Valve Corporation, https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/ |
| Apple App Store (iOS, iPadOS) | iCloud / Game Center (saved-game sync, achievements, leaderboards if enabled) | Apple Inc., https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ |
| Google Play (Android) | Google Play Games services (saved games, achievements) | Google LLC, https://policies.google.com/privacy |

The platforms may process data such as your account name, device identifiers and play data under their own terms. You can turn these features off in the platform settings, and the game remains fully playable offline. Purchases are processed solely by the platform; we receive aggregated sales reports without personal data about individual buyers.

### 5. Technical data and third-party code

The game uses the Unity engine. We have not enabled Unity analytics, Unity ads or other Unity online services in the game. The game does not contact our servers or third-party servers on its own, except for the platform features in section 4. *[Verify before release by network traffic inspection and SDK audit; see compliance checklist.]*

### 6. Legal basis

Because we do not process personal data, no legal basis is needed for our own processing. Platform providers process data on their own legal basis (typically performance of the contract with you and their legitimate interests).

### 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for example the authority of your place of residence, or the authority responsible for us: [COMPETENT STATE DATA PROTECTION AUTHORITY, to be confirmed]). Since we hold no personal data about you, we usually cannot identify you in order to fulfil requests. Please contact the platform provider for data they hold. You can contact us at [EMAIL] any time.

### 8. Children

The game is not directed at collecting data from anyone, including children. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

### 9. Changes

If we change the game in a way that affects privacy (for example adding online features), we will update this policy before the change takes effect and state the date above.

### 2. Kurzfassung

BRASSLIGHT ist ein Offline-Spiel. **Wir erheben, speichern auf eigenen Servern, teilen oder verkaufen keine personenbezogenen Daten.** Das Spiel hat keine Benutzerkonten, keine Werbung, keine In-App-Käufe, keine Analyse- und keine Tracking-Funktionen. Wir betreiben keine Spieleserver.

Das Spiel speichert Spielfortschritt, Einstellungen (z. B. Sprache, Lautstärke, Steuerung, Grafikqualität) und Freischaltungen in einer lokalen Speicherdatei auf Ihrem Gerät. Diese Daten werden nicht an uns übermittelt und wir haben keinen Zugriff darauf. Sie werden gelöscht, wenn Sie das Spiel deinstallieren oder die Speicherdatei löschen. Ob das Betriebssystem diese Datei in Geräte-Backups aufnimmt, bestimmen Ihre Geräteeinstellungen.

Das Spiel kann Funktionen des Stores bzw. der Plattform nutzen, über die Sie es erworben haben. Diese werden vom jeweiligen Plattformbetreiber betrieben, **nicht von uns**. Wir erhalten von dort keine personenbezogenen Daten. Es gelten die Datenschutzerklärungen der Anbieter:

|-----------|-------------------|------------------------|

| Apple App Store (iOS, iPadOS) | iCloud / Game Center (Spielstand-Sync, Erfolge, ggf. Bestenlisten) | Apple Inc., https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ |

Käufe werden ausschließlich von der Plattform abgewickelt; wir erhalten nur aggregierte Verkaufsberichte ohne personenbezogene Angaben einzelner Käufer.

Das Spiel nutzt die Unity-Engine. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads oder andere Online-Dienste von Unity sind im Spiel nicht aktiviert.
