# Impressum Template (Germany) — BRASSLIGHT

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. Have it reviewed** by a lawyer or the IT-Recht service you use, and check it with the Steuerberater (VAT ID, business form).
> Date verified: 2026-11-08. Legal basis: the Impressum duty moved from **§ 5 TMG** to **§ 5 DDG (Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz)** on 14 May 2024 (TMG repealed/replaced). Do **not** cite "§ 5 TMG". Sources checked: IHK and law-firm summaries (e.g. https://www.mth-partner.de/impressumspflicht-nach-dem-ddg-rechtssicheres-impressum-erstellen/). The statute text itself (gesetze-im-internet.de, "DDG § 5") was not fetched in this session, so confirm the item list there.

## When you need it

- Required for **commercial ("geschäftsmäßig") digital services** aimed at Germany: your game website / landing page, a press kit page, a social profile, a dev blog.
- Requirement: easily recognisable, directly reachable, permanently available (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG). Label the link **"Impressum"**, one click from every page, and from the store pages' "website"/"support" link target.
- Steam, Google Play and the App Store pages themselves are the platforms' services; still, link to your site's Impressum and privacy policy from them.
- Privacy: the address you publish here is **public**. Consider a business address or c/o / Impressum-service address (see compliance checklist, DSA section). A P.O. box is generally **not** acceptable for the Impressum (needs a "ladungsfähige Anschrift"; confirm with a lawyer).

## Notes for the owner and Steuerberater

| Item | Note |
|------|------|
| Items required for everyone (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1, 2 DDG) | Name and ladungsfähige postal address; email plus a second means of fast direct contact (phone or contact form) |
| Register entry (Nr. 4) | Only if entered in Handelsregister etc. (e.g. e. K., GmbH, UG). Add register court and number |
| Supervisory authority / chamber (Nr. 3, 5) | Normally not applicable to game sales; Kleingewerbe/Gewerbe is not a regulated profession |
| USt-IdNr. (Nr. 6) | Only if you have one. With Kleinunternehmerregelung (§ 19 UStG) you usually have none. Whether to apply for one (needed for some EU B2B/platform dealings) is a Steuerberater question |
| Kleinunternehmer note | If you use § 19 UStG, invoices need a note; a website note is not required for a game site, but ask the Steuerberater |
| Gewerbeanmeldung | Selling games commercially normally needs a Gewerbeanmeldung (Gewerbeamt) or, for freelance work, a Finanzamt classification. Business-form question for the Steuerberater and Gewerbeamt |
| EU OS platform | I recall it was discontinued in July 2025; **not verified this session**. Check before publishing the dispute-resolution wording |
| Consistency | Same name and address as in the privacy policy, Apple/Google trader details and Steam business info, unless you deliberately use a different public contact address |
| Fonts/CDN | Do not load Google Fonts or other third-party resources from the site without updating the website privacy notice (the site needs its own web privacy text, separate from the game policy) |
