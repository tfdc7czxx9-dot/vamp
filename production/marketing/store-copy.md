## 0. Rules for this copy (internal)

- IP: no "HD-2D", no game names, no "like X". Style is called "2D-3D diorama" or "pixel art in 3D worlds". Verne and Wells are named as *inspiration* only; we do not claim to adapt a novel. No Disney Nautilus, no known film tripod/Morlock designs (`design/ip-guidelines.md`).
- Copy describes the **1.0 launch scope** from `design/gdd/game-concept.md` (4 worlds, 6 leaders, etc.). **Nothing here may go live until the claims gate (section 7) is checked against the shipping build.** The MVP currently contains only World 1. Before the Coming Soon page (end of Phase 3) use the "vertical slice" variant noted in section 7.
- No unverifiable superlatives ("best", "first", "only"). No review quotes until we have real ones.
- No platform names on the wrong store (no "Steam Deck" on mobile, no "Play Games" on iOS).

### 1.2 Long description (About This Game)

**EN**

```
Your machine rattles. The swarm is coming. You move, everything else happens on its own.

BRASSLIGHT is a brass-and-steam survivor-like roguelite set in the impossible worlds of 19th-century science fiction. Lead an expedition to the centre of the Earth, the ocean floor, the Moon and a Martian-invaded London. Your gadgets fire automatically. Your job is to stay alive, gather Aether and Brass, and decide where to stand.

[h2]Recruit the monsters you defeat[/h2]
[list]
[*] Defeat an Elite and it leaves a stunned beast or a wrecked machine, marked by a glowing ring.
[*] Stand inside the ring to recruit it. It joins your crew as a Companion with its own attack pattern.
[*] Companions follow you in formation and level up with you.
[*] Crew space is limited. Replace a companion, or scrap the remnant for building materials.
[*] The catch: recruiting means standing still in the middle of the swarm.
[/list]

[h2]Build on the battlefield[/h2]
[list]
[*] Enemies drop Brass, a second resource next to experience.
[*] Stand on a Build Plot to raise a structure: barricades, steam turrets, mine fields, lightning masts, lure beacons, field workshops and more.
[*] Return to a structure to upgrade it. Structures can be destroyed, so defend them.
[*] Your crew guards your fortifications, and the workshop repairs your crew.
[/list]

[h2]Fortress or caravan?[/h2]
Hold a fortified position, or roam the map with a big crew. Every expedition ends up looking and playing differently.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Four voyages inspired by public-domain classics: the Hollow Earth, the ocean depths, the Martian invasion and the Lunar caverns
[*] Six original expedition leaders with their own starting gadget and passive
[*] Dozens of auto-firing gadgets with evolutions
[*] A bestiary of recruitable creatures and machines
[*] Runs of 15 to 20 minutes, with permanent expedition upgrades between voyages
[*] Pixel-art characters in lit 3D dioramas: tilt-shift depth of field, bloom, lantern light
[*] Original orchestral score with Victorian brass colors
[*] Play with keyboard and mouse, gamepad or touch, using movement only
[*] Playable offline. Pay once: no ads, no in-app purchases, no energy timers
[/list]

Inspired by the novels of Jules Verne and H. G. Wells. All characters, creatures and machines in the game are original designs.
```
