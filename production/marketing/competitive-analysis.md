# Competitive Analysis: Survivor-likes / Bullet Heaven

> Owner: `store-marketing-specialist`. Internal only. Competitor names may appear here but **never** in store copy, tags text, trailers or press material (`design/ip-guidelines.md`).
> **Research date: 2026-11-08.** Prices come from web search snippets (third-party trackers, blogs, store pages). They move with sales and region. Items marked *(verify)* must be re-checked on the live store page before any pricing decision is locked.

## 1. Market snapshot

- Steam added an official **Bullet Heaven** tag in May 2026, separate from the older "Bullet Hell" tag. Early tagged titles include Vampire Survivors, Brotato, Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor, The Spell Brigade, Megabonk and Jotunnslayer. Source: [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/valve-steam-tags-bullet-heaven-vampire-survivors/), [Play Day One](https://playday.one/2026/05/20/bullet-heaven-games-finally-recognised-as-a-genre/).
  - Implication: a browsable tag now exists, which helps discovery for a new entrant, and it also makes the genre feel crowded. We must be tagged and described so that players who browse it see "steampunk + crew + building" within one glance.
- The genre is price-compressed. Top premium entries sit at roughly 5 to 10 USD/EUR. Almost all are priced low and rely on discounts and word of mouth.
- Saturated: fantasy/horror/gothic settings, "one more weapon" upgrade loops, 8-bit/16-bit sprite looks, sci-fi space shooters, roguelite meta-trees.

## 3. Positioning map

| Dimension | Saturated | Where BRASSLIGHT sits |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Setting | Gothic, fantasy, zombies, space | Victorian retro-sci-fi from public-domain Verne/Wells worlds. Only hobby-scale itch.io projects and one small Steam title touch steampunk/submarine. **Appears unoccupied at quality level.** |
| Look | Flat 2D sprites; some 3D low-poly | Pixel-art sprites in lit 3D dioramas (tilt-shift, bloom). Visually distinct in thumbnails |
| Pace | 15 to 30 min runs | 15 to 20 min runs, mobile-friendly |
| Control | Move-only is standard | Move-only, but recruiting and building are done by *standing* in zones |
| Business model | Premium on PC; many free-with-ads mobile clones | Premium everywhere, no ads, no IAP. A clear mobile differentiator |

## 4. Mechanics comparison

| Mechanic | Vampire Survivors-style baseline | Brotato | DRG: Survivor | BRASSLIGHT |
|----------|---------------------------------|---------|---------------|------------|
| Auto-attack | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Summons / temporary allies | common | limited | some | yes |
| Turrets / deployables | rare | **yes (Engineer class)** | **yes** | yes, as one of several structures |
| Permanent structures placed on map plots (upgradeable, destructible, with a second resource) | no | no | no | **yes (Build Plots + Brass)** |
| Converting defeated elites into permanent crew (stand in ring to recruit, keeps enemy attack pattern, levels with you, crew cap, scrap alternative) | no | no | no | **yes (Recruit)** |
| Synergy between crew and structures | no | no | no | yes (Workshop repairs crew; evolutions that combine both) |

Honest reading: turrets are **not** new. Summons are common. What appears new is the combination of (a) turning defeated elites into a persistent, named crew and (b) field building on fixed plots, both tied to the tension of standing still in a swarm. This is based on our search only, not an exhaustive scan of Steam. Do not write "first" or "only" in public copy. Use "recruit" and "build" claims in the neutral form ("Recruit defeated elites").

## 5. Differentiation lines we can back up (candidate hooks)

1. "Beat the elite, then recruit it." (Recruit)
2. "Bolt a fortress together under fire." (Engineering)
3. "Verne and Wells, in a glowing diorama." (setting plus look)
4. "Pay once. No ads. No in-app purchases." (mobile trust)

## 6. Risks for marketing

- Thumbnail test: at small capsule size the swarm genre looks identical. The capsule must show brass, steam, and a creature crew, not just sprites and explosions.
- Genre fatigue: wishlist growth needs a clear hook within 3 seconds in the trailer (see launch plan).
- Mobile: free ad-supported clones dominate search. Premium needs a short, clear "no ads" message in the first screenshot caption.
- Claims discipline: the Recruit and Engineering hook is unproven until the Phase 1 playtest. Do not publish store pages before the go/pivot gate (roadmap 1.4).
