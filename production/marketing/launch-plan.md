## 1. Gating principles

1. No public page before the title trademark search clears (roadmap 0.7) and the Phase 1 go/pivot gate passes (roadmap 1.4). A hook that does not play well should not be advertised.
2. The Coming Soon page opens when the vertical slice is playable and shows real footage (end of Phase 3).
3. A Steam demo needs a stable, short build; a weak demo at a festival hurts wishlists more than no demo.
4. Store copy follows the claims gate in `store-copy.md`.

## 3. Pricing recommendation

### 3.1 Options

| Option | Steam | Mobile | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-------|--------|------|------|
| A (concept default) | 4.99 | 3.99 | Low barrier for a new IP with no audience; matches Brotato mobile Premium at 4.99 USD and Submarine Survivor at about 4.99 on Steam | Signals "small game"; little room for discounts |
| B | 6.99 | 3.99 to 4.99 | Reflects 4 worlds and 6 leaders; Halls of Torment is at 6.66 USD | Higher barrier without a wishlist base |
| C | 9.99 | 4.99 | Megabonk sold well at 9.99 USD | Needs a breakout, visibly larger content |

Prices are per `competitive-analysis.md` (several *(verify)*).

### 3.2 Recommendation

- **Go with A at launch (Steam 4.99 EUR / 4.99 USD; mobile 3.99 EUR / 3.99 USD) if 1.0 ships with the planned 4 worlds, and re-open the decision at the Next Fest results.** If wishlists exceed the target and playtest retention is strong, move to B (6.99) before the release date is set. A raise is easier before launch than after.
- Rationale: new IP, no franchise audience, a genre where players expect low prices, and the premium-vs-free-with-ads gap on mobile is the key selling point. Price is "no ads, no IAP" at a fair, single price.
- Do not raise the price after a free update pushes the content up: keep a price floor commitment instead. Free DLC worlds (Time Machine, Moreau; concept) are a goodwill tool.
- Mobile at 3.99 sits one step below Steam to reflect lower session time and store cut parity.

### 3.3 Launch discount

- 10 to 15 percent for 7 to 14 days (Steam launch discount rules above). Recommend **15 percent, 14 days**. Add 20 to 30 percent in the first regular sale after day 30. Never discount below 50 percent in year one.
- Bundle or "franchise" discounts only when a second product exists.
- Mobile: no launch discount on the paid tier at first; use the store's price-promotion tool *(verify)* only if sales stall, to avoid training users to wait.

### 3.4 Regional pricing

- Use the store's recommended regional price tables as a base, then review these cases manually: Turkey, Argentina, Brazil, India, Southeast Asia, CIS, China (lower purchasing power, high piracy risk), and the EUR zone (rounding to x.99). Steam shows a "recommended prices" list in Steamworks *(verify)*; Google Play and App Store tiers/automatic conversions *(verify)*.
- Currencies: set EUR and USD explicitly; let the regional tables handle the rest. Keep the EUR price tax-inclusive in the EU (VAT shown by stores); check with the Steuerberater how net revenue is booked.
- Check the price ladder against rounding: x.49 or x.99 only; do not pick prices that land at 3.97 after tax conversion.
- Keep Steam and mobile within one price tier to avoid "why is it cheaper there" threads.

## 4. Press kit outline (`production/marketing/press-kit.md`)

Folder structure and content:

1. Fact sheet: title, developer, release date, platforms, price, languages (EN, DE, FR if shipped), age rating, website, contact, engine credit as required.
2. Short pitch (one line, 3 lines, 100 words) in EN and DE.
3. Description and feature list (reuse `store-copy.md`; pass the claims gate).
4. Logo pack: vector + PNG, light/dark.
5. Key art and capsules (sizes in `asset-specs.md`).
6. 10 to 15 screenshots at 1920 x 1080, clean, no UI mockups, with captions.
7. GIFs: recruit ring, building a turret, boss.
8. Trailer links (YouTube, direct MP4) and b-roll.
9. Credits and team bios (owner approves what is public).
10. Credit line: "Inspired by the novels of Jules Verne and H. G. Wells."
11. Review-key request form and embargo rules.
12. Social links, contact email, privacy-friendly press page.

Style rule: no competitor names, no "HD-2D", no "first"/"only" claims.

## 5. Trailer brief

- **Length**: 60 to 90 seconds main trailer, plus a 20 to 30 second cut for mobile stores and social.
- **First 5 seconds**: real gameplay, a swarm closing in with turrets firing and the crew visible. No logo animation, no black screen, no text walls.
- **Structure**: 0 to 5 hook (swarm + crew + turret); 5 to 20 recruit (elite falls, ring, crew grows); 20 to 40 build (stand on plot, structure appears, shot of fortress holding); 40 to 60 worlds and variety; 60 to 75 evolution or boss peak; last 5 to 10 seconds: logo, "Extraordinary Expeditions", platforms and release window.
- **Mobile-safe framing**: keep action and the player within the centre 80 percent of a 16:9 frame; text large enough to read on a phone; burn-in subtitles for EN, DE captions as a separate track.
- **Platform rules**: Steam trailer may show Steam logos only per Steam rules; the Play and App Store versions must not show Steam, Xbox or PlayStation UI or other stores' logos, and the PC version must not show touch joystick UI. Produce three cuts: PC, Android, iOS.
- **Audio**: original score only; no sound or music resembling protected adaptations (`ip-guidelines.md`).
- **Content**: gameplay must be real captured footage from the build, no enhanced or faked features, no unreleased content unless labeled "in development" in the video.

## 6. Creator outreach approach

1. **List building (L-9)**: 150 to 300 creators in three tiers: micro (under 10k followers), mid (10k to 100k), larger (100k+) in survivor-like, roguelite, steampunk/Victorian aesthetic, cozy-chaos, and mobile-game niches. Include DE-language creators and Steam Deck-focused channels.
2. **Principles**: personal, short mails; one line hook, link to the page and trailer, no pressure or required positive coverage; say plainly that keys come with no obligations. Follow disclosure rules (creators must label sponsored/free copies; we never pay for or script reviews).
3. **Assets**: a creator page with logo, screenshots, b-roll, and a "recruit and build" talking-point sheet that sticks to verified claims.
4. **Keys**: Steam keys through Steamworks key requests *(verify limits)*; mobile access via TestFlight codes and Play closed-test or promo codes *(verify)*. Keys sent at L-3 to L-2 with an embargo time of launch day.
5. **Demo outreach**: ping creators at Next Fest start with the demo link and a streamer-friendly "short run" tip.
6. **Press**: pitch genre and trade outlets at L-6 (announcement with trailer), L-2 (review keys), and launch day. German-language outlets in parallel (Impressum and German contact line).
7. **Community**: Discord and Steam Community hub managed by `community-manager`; weekly dev posts; "recruit of the week" fan content.
8. **Tracking**: spreadsheet per creator (contact, key sent, date, video link). Respond to everyone; thank publicly.
9. **Budget**: no paid sponsorships assumed; any paid campaign needs an owner decision and proper disclosure.

## 7. ASO plan (mobile)

- Keywords: start with the sets in `store-copy.md`; review monthly with store analytics *(owner access needed)*.
- Google Play store listing experiments: test icon first, then feature graphic, then first two screenshots; run each at least 7 days with enough traffic *(verify minimums)*.
- App Store product page optimization: test icon, screenshots, preview video order; changes must go through review.
- Re-localize screenshots for EN and DE; add FR if it ships.
- Ratings: ask for a rating only after a completed first expedition, via the native prompt, never on a loss screen.

## 8. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Recruit/Build not fun in prototype | Do not open the page; pivot per roadmap gate |
| Low wishlists at L-6 | Move release to a later window; do not release into a crowded week |
| Genre fatigue | Lead with steampunk Verne/Wells look and crew-building hook in capsule and trailer |
| Trademark conflict on BRASSLIGHT | Fallback title list from `creative-director` before Coming Soon page |
| Mobile free-with-ads clones outrank us | Rely on Steam audience, creator content, and clean "premium, no ads" positioning |
| Claims drift between store copy and build | Claims gate in `store-copy.md`, checked by release-manager at L-1 week |
